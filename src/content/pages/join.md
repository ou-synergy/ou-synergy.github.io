### Fully funded Ph.D. opportunities at the University of Oklahoma

Are you passionate about advancing the frontiers of artificial intelligence and translating
cutting-edge research into meaningful real-world impact?

I am seeking up to three motivated and intellectually curious Ph.D. students to join my
research lab at the University of Oklahoma, with funded positions beginning in Spring or Fall
2027.

Our research explores exciting opportunities at the intersection of AI innovation and
societal impact:

- **Agentic AI for software engineering.** Building intelligent, autonomous agents that
  transform how software is designed, developed, and maintained.
- **Efficient and scalable generative AI.** Making large language models faster, more
  resource-efficient, affordable, and accessible.
- **AI for healthcare.** Harnessing artificial intelligence to address complex healthcare
  challenges and improve human well-being.
- **AI for education.** Reimagining teaching and learning through intelligent, adaptive, and
  personalized AI technologies.

I welcome candidates with strong technical foundations, intellectual curiosity, and a passion
for tackling challenging research problems. Preference will be given to applicants with a
master's degree.

**Interested in joining us?** Please email me at [zeshili@ou.edu](mailto:zeshili@ou.edu) with
your CV and a brief description of your research interests.

Please share this opportunity with prospective students in your network!

### Current undergraduates or master's students

If you are an OU student looking for research experience, email with a couple of sentences
about what you are curious about.
