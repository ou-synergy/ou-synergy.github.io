title: "Join our lab"
---

We are always glad to hear from people who want to do novel research. The lab
works on all areas of AI for software engineering and human–AI collaboration, including energy efficient AI systems, AI for healthcare, agentic AI systems, etc. You do not need to have settled on one area to reach out.

**We have one funded PhD opening for Spring 2027.** If this looks like a fit, please email
us at [zeshili@ou.edu](mailto:zeshili@ou.edu) with a short note about what you have
worked on and what you would like to work on next.

### Current undergraduates or master's students

If you are an OU student looking for research experience, email with a couple of sentences
about what you are curious about. We try to scope first projects so you can see a result
within a semester.
