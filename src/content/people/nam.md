I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Oklahoma, advised by Dr. Ze Shi Li. My research focuses on software engineering, large language models, and AI-assisted methods for understanding and improving developer work. I am especially interested in how AI can support empirical software engineering research, code generation, data mining, and developer-facing tools. More broadly, my work explores how human-AI collaboration can help researchers and practitioners analyze complex software engineering problems more effectively.
