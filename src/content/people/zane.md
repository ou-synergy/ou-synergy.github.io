Dr. Ze Shi Li directs the Synergy Lab in the School of Computer Science at the University of Oklahoma, where his research sits at the intersection of AI for software engineering and human–AI collaboration. He builds AI tools and systems designed to work for the people who use them. He aims to tackle the hard problems of efficiency, usability, trust, and adoption that determine how AI delivers positive benefits to end users.

He also serves as co-chair of OU's [Software Studio](https://softwarestudio.cs.ou.edu), the university's premier engineering and entrepreneurship program, where students go from idea to launched venture.

Dr. Li earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Victoria.

He is actively recruiting motivated students who want to help shape the next generation of AI tools.
